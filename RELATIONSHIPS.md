# Paths of Ruin — Relationship System

## Overview

Every character has relationship arcs that develop through camp interactions and battle proximity. Relationships drive camp scenes, party synergy, and replayability.

---

## Support Levels

| Level | Description | Trigger |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **C** | Initial meeting / casual interaction | Characters deployed together in battle OR adjacent in camp |
| **B** | Deeper conversation / shared experience | More battles together + camp proximity |
| **A** | Intimate confession / bond sealed | Maximum support; triggers unique scene |

**Progression:** C → B → A (sequential, cannot skip)

---

## Relationship Types

### 1. Family Bonds

Family relationships (Yuki/Suki, Mei/Wei, Brother Leo/Sister Maria) have dedicated C/B/A support chains.

- **C:** Awkward reunion / sibling rivalry / religious debate
- **B:** Shared memory / old wound / understanding deepens
- **A:** Acceptance / reconciliation / "You're still my family"

### 2. Romantic Relationships

Each character has **two possible romantic partners** among the 19 heroes.

**Conflict Rule:** Multiple characters can pursue the same person, but that person can only reach **A-rank with ONE** of them. The other remains at B-rank max.

Example:
- Wei → Roland (can reach A)
- Mei → Roland (can reach A)
- Roland can only A-rank **one** of them. The other stays at B.

**MC Romance:** The player character (Apostle) has **3 romanceable characters** among the 19. These are the only romantic relationships that can include the MC.

## Camp Scene Triggers

- **Family C/B/A scenes:** Trigger when both family members are present in camp and support level requirements are met
- **Romantic scenes:** Trigger when both characters are present and support level is high enough
- **Conflict scenes:** Trigger when two characters pursuing the same person are both present
- **Corruption reactions:** Special scenes when a corrupted hero reunites with their romantic interest on the other side
- **Cross-faction romances:** Romantic scenes with enemy-side characters (e.g., MC ↔ Twin Converter) **ONLY unlock after that character is corrupted AND at least one full map has passed.** The delay represents them processing the conversion before any romantic content begins.

### Twin Converter — Special Case

The Twin Converter starts on the **human/Goddess's side**. All romantic content with them follows these rules:
- **Same-side romances** (Elena, Sister Maria, Tomás): Available normally — they're already allies
- **Cross-faction romance** (MC): **ONLY unlocks after corrupting the Twin Converter AND completing at least one full map.** The delay represents them processing the conversion before romance begins.
- This makes the Twin Converter a **strategic AND romantic target** — but you have to wait for the payoff.
