# Paths of Ruin — The 19 Heroes (Draft)

## Design Notes
- **One Divine Blessing per character** (no separate unique ability)
- **Full kit:** Base Class ability + Advanced Class ability + Divine Blessing
- **Minimalist:** Simple ideas, cool variations, rewarding team building
- **Weapon/item uses, not MP**

---

## Fencer (2 heroes)

### Yuki (Female, Fencer → Hero/Assassin)
- **Classroom:** Student council president. Polished, organized, expects excellence.
- **Divine Blessing: Riposte** — When an enemy misses attacking her, she immediately counterattacks with +20% crit.
- **Personality:** Believes in order and hierarchy. The Goddess's system makes *sense* to her. Corruption feels like chaos.
- **Corruption reaction:** Outrage. "How could you side with *them*?" Treats it as a personal betrayal.
- **Relationship hooks:** Admires the Knight's discipline; clashes with the Thief. **Cousin to Suki** — family reunions are tense (both rigid, different expressions).

### Rafael (Male, Fencer → Hero/Assassin)
- **Classroom:** Drama kid. Flamboyant, theatrical, always performing.
- **Divine Blessing: Flourish** — After defeating an enemy, adjacent allies gain +10% crit for 1 turn.
- **Personality:** Lives for the spotlight. Being a "hero" is the role of a lifetime. Genuinely enjoys combat as spectacle.
- **Corruption reaction:** Conflicted. The Demon King's side is *also* theatrical. He's curious if he'd get bigger scenes.
- **Relationship hooks:** Best friends with the Bard; performs duets in camp.

## Thief (2 heroes)

### Marcus (Male, Thief → Assassin/Pirate)
- **Classroom:** The troublemaker. Detention regular, bored not malicious.
- **Divine Blessing: Second Hand** — Once per battle, steal a non-weapon item from an enemy on attack.
- **Personality:** Snarky, improvises, hates authority. Goddess's orders chafe.
- **Corruption reaction:** "New boss, same deal. You paying better?" Surprisingly adaptable.
- **Relationship hooks:** Rivals with Yuki (she caught him skipping); respects the Bard's hustle.

### Aisha (Female, Thief → Assassin/Pirate)
- **Classroom:** Invisible. Nobody remembers her name. Slips through crowds.
- **Divine Blessing: Smokestep** — After attacking, may move 2 spaces without provoking attacks of opportunity.
- **Personality:** Observant, patient, speaks rarely. Being noticed is uncomfortable.
- **Corruption reaction:** Relief? On the Demon King's side, being unseen is an asset, not a failure.
- **Relationship hooks:** Secretly leaves notes for the Priest; the only one who knows his real fears.

---

## Fighter (2 heroes)

### Greta (Female, Fighter → Barbarian/Pirate)
- **Classroom:** Wrestling team captain. Direct, competitive, protective of underdogs.
- **Divine Blessing: Overwhelm** — If her attack reduces an enemy below 50% HP, she may attack again (once per turn).
- **Personality:** Respects strength above all. The Goddess is strong. The Demon King is strong. She just wants to know which side *wins*.
- **Corruption reaction:** Respects your strength for beating her. "Fine. I'll follow the stronger one."
- **Relationship hooks:** Sparring partner with the Knight; mentors the Cavalier.

### Tomás (Male, Fighter → Barbarian/Pirate)
- **Classroom:** Quietly built kid. Nobody expected him to be strong. He didn't either.
- **Divine Blessing: Breaker** — Attacks against enemies with full HP deal +30% damage.
- **Personality:** Surprised by his own power. Still learning to trust it. Hates hurting people.
- **Corruption reaction:** Devastated. He wanted to be a shield, not a weapon. Being on the "monster" side breaks him.
- **Relationship hooks:** Protective of the Mage; the Priest is his confessor.

---

## Knight (2 heroes)

### Roland (Male, Knight → Dragoon/Barbarian)
- **Classroom:** ROTC kid. Disciplined, patriotic, believes in duty.
- **Divine Blessing: Bulwark** — Adjacent allies take 25% less damage from physical attacks.
- **Personality:** "The Goddess chose us for a reason." Utterly committed. Not blind, just *chosen*.
- **Corruption reaction:** Despair. His faith was everything. Without it, he doesn't know who he is.
- **Relationship hooks:** Best friend with Keith; rivals with Greta (different philosophies of strength).

### Suki (Female, Knight → Dragoon/Barbarian)
- **Classroom:** History nerd. Obsessed with samurai, knights, honor codes.
- **Divine Blessing: Stand Ground** — If she does not move this turn, gains +15 DEF and can counterattack at range 1.
- **Personality:** Lives by a code she wrote herself. The Goddess's structure validates it.
- **Corruption reaction:** Fascination. The Demon King has *his own* code. She wants to understand it.
- **Relationship hooks:** Debates history with the Mage; the Bard writes songs about her. **Cousin to Yuki** — same family, diverged into student council vs samurai obsession.

---

## Cavalier (2 heroes)

### Keith (Male, Cavalier → Dragoon/Paladin)
- **Classroom:** The jock. Varsity track, popular, oblivious but not cruel.
- **Divine Blessing: Wind Rider** — After moving 4+ spaces in a straight line, next attack deals +50% damage.
- **Personality:** Confident, loyal, thinks this is a heroic quest. Believes the Goddess is good because everyone says so.
- **Corruption reaction:** Confusion. "Why me? I'm one of the good guys." Then anger at the betrayal.
- **Relationship hooks:** Best friends with Roland; competitive with Greta.

### Mei (Female, Cavalier → Dragoon/Paladin)
- **Classroom:** Equestrian club. Quiet, precise, animal lover.
- **Divine Blessing: Trample** — After moving through an enemy's space (overrun), they take damage equal to her MOVE stat.
- **Personality:** Gentle outside combat, ruthless in it. Her mount is her partner, not her vehicle.
- **Corruption reaction:** Grief. Her mount rejects her at first. She has to re-earn its trust.
- **Relationship hooks:** The only one the animals trust; bonded with Tomás (both gentle souls). **Older sister to Wei** — she escaped into horses because he was always exploding things.

---

## Priest (2 heroes)

### Brother Leo (Male, Priest → Paladin/Sage)
- **Classroom:** Youth group leader. Sincerely kind, a little naive, genuinely believes in helping people.
- **Divine Blessing: Mend** — Once per turn, heal an adjacent ally for 30% of their max HP. Cannot heal self.
- **Personality:** The world is broken and he's here to fix it. Corruption is another form of brokenness he wants to understand.
- **Corruption reaction:** Not anger — *sadness*. "What happened to you? What did they do?"
- **Relationship hooks:** Confessor to half the class; secretly afraid he's not good enough.

### Sister Maria (Female, Priest → Paladin/Sage)
- **Classroom:** Debate team captain. Sharp tongue, sharper mind. Argued her way out of detention every time.
- **Divine Blessing: Rebuke** — When an enemy attacks an ally adjacent to her, she retaliates with light damage equal to her RES.
- **Personality:** Righteous anger as a virtue. The Goddess gives her permission to be furious at injustice.
- **Corruption reaction:** Screaming rage. "How DARE you choose the monsters over us?"
- **Relationship hooks:** Debates theology with Brother Leo; the Warlock is her intellectual rival.

---

## Mage (2 heroes)

### Elena (Female, Mage → Archmage/Sage)
- **Classroom:** The quiet one. Sits in the back, reads during lunch.
- **Divine Blessing: Spellweave** — When attacking with a tome, may consume a second tome from inventory. Attack gains the second tome's element/effect. Consumed tome is destroyed.
- **Personality:** Sharp, observant, emotionally distant. Treats this world like a puzzle.
- **Corruption reaction:** Calculation. Weighs the odds. If you can offer better access to magic...
- **Relationship hooks:** Secretly tutors Marcus in math; respects Sister Maria's intellect.

### Wei (Male, Mage → Archmage/Sage)
- **Classroom:** Science fair winner. "Magic is just physics we don't understand yet."
- **Divine Blessing: Overcharge** — May double a tome's damage by rolling twice for the attack (take the lower hit roll). If it misses, the tome is destroyed.
- **Personality:** Curious to a fault. Wants to dissect everything. Including you.
- **Corruption reaction:** Scientific interest. "Your power is fascinating. I want to study it."
- **Relationship hooks:** Rivals with Wei intellectually; the Bard thinks he's insufferable.

---

## Warlock (2 heroes)

### Damien (Male, Warlock → Archmage/Shaman)
- **Classroom:** Goth kid. Wore black before it was cool. Actually just likes Edgar Allan Poe.
- **Divine Blessing: Life Tap** — Sacrifice 10% max HP to deal +50% damage on next attack.
- **Personality:** Dramatic, self-aware about it. Secretly insecure. Wants to be taken seriously.
- **Corruption reaction:** Bitter acceptance. "Of course the demon side wants me. Figures."
- **Relationship hooks:** Writes terrible poetry with the Bard; Brother Leo is kind to him.

### Nia (Female, Warlock → Archmage/Shaman)
- **Classroom:** Class president's rival. Ambitious, ruthless, will do what it takes.
- **Divine Blessing: Soul Bind** — After defeating an enemy, their remaining HP is added to her next attack's damage (decays after 2 turns).
- **Personality:** The ends justify the means. The Goddess is a means to power. So is the Demon King.
- **Corruption reaction:** Transactional. "What do you offer? Make it worth my time."
- **Relationship hooks:** Rivals with Yuki; respects Elena's intellect. **Younger brother to Mei** — thinks science is the only thing that makes sense.

---

## Bard (2 heroes)

### Jax (Male, Bard → Saint/Shaman)
- **Classroom:** Band kid. Guitar, drums, anything with strings. Always humming.
- **Divine Blessing: Battle Hymn** — At the start of turn, all allies within 3 spaces gain +2 SPD until next turn.
- **Personality:** Optimistic, connects people, believes music is universal. Hates silence.
- **Corruption reaction:** Tries to *reach* you. "You're still in there, right? The person I knew?"
- **Relationship hooks:** Performs with Rafael; writes songs about Suki.

### Lila (Female, Bard → Saint/Shaman)
- **Classroom:** Social media queen. Everyone follows her, nobody knows her.
- **Divine Blessing: Mockingbird** — Can copy the last action used by an ally (attack, skill, or item use) with -20% effectiveness.
- **Personality:** Chameleon. Reflects what people want to see. Exhausted by it.
- **Corruption reaction:** Curiosity. On your side, she doesn't have to perform. She can just... be.
- **Relationship hooks:** Everyone's "friend" but nobody's close; Aisha sees through her.

---

## MC Replacement (The Twin Converter)

### Twin Converter (Gender: opposite of MC)
- **Base Class:** Varies (the class the MC would have been)
- **Divine Blessing: Purge** — Can cleanse Corruption from one unit per battle, reverting them to the Goddess's side.
- **Role:** The antagonist's countermeasure. The arms race incarnate.
- **If corrupted:** You now have **two converters**. The strategic balance tips entirely.

*Total: 19 heroes + 1 MC = 20 students. 10 male, 10 female.*
