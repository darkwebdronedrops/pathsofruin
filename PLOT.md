# Paths of Ruin — Plot Structure

## Core Principle: Heroes Are the Plot

The story is delivered through **hero encounters**, not cutscenes or narration. Every hero carries plot-relevant dialogue that triggers at specific moments:

- **Pre-combat dialogue** — Why they're here, what they know, how they feel about the war
- **Mid-combat banter** — Reacting to your tactics, calling out corrupted allies, revealing relationships
- **Post-combat dialogue** — Victory/defeat reactions, recruitment offers, plot revelations

**Skipping a hero means skipping their plot contribution.** The story adapts to who's present, who's corrupted, who's dead.

## Plot Delivery by Hero

### Tutorial Heroes (Chapters 1-3)

These heroes establish the world and the war:

**Tutorial Hero 1 (Chapter 2) — First Monster Combat**
- Explains the Demon King's realm
- Introduces Apostle's Touch
- Establishes the "chosen by opposite gods" premise

**Tutorial Hero 2 (Chapter 3) — First Corruption**
- First encounter with a Goddess hero
- Demonstrates the corruption mechanic
- Reacts to being corrupted (sets tone for all future reactions)

### Twin Converter — Central Plot Device

The Twin Converter is not just an enemy — they're the **key to the entire story**:

- **Phase 1 (Avoid, Chapters 1-10):** Rumors about them. Other heroes mention "the other chosen one." Mystery builds.
- **Phase 2 (Random, Chapters 11-15):** First direct encounters. They know things about you that no one should know. They ask questions that cut deep.
- **Phase 3 (Pursuit, Chapter 16+):** The confrontation. The truth: you and the Twin Converter are **two halves of the same blessing**. The Demon King and Goddess each got one Apostle. Neither is complete without the other.

**The Choice:** Corrupt the Twin Converter → gain their power, unlock the true ending. Kill them → win the war, lose the truth. Let them live → they keep uncorrupting your army.

## Dynamic Plot Adaptation

The story changes based on who you've encountered:

### Example: The War's Cost
- If you met **Greta** → you know about destroyed villages
- If you met **Mei** → you know about the environmental damage
- If you met **Brother Leo** → you know about refugee camps
- If you met NONE of them → the war is abstract, just numbers

### Example: The Goddess's Truth
- If you met **Roland** → you know the religious justification
- If you met **Sister Maria** → you know the fanatical edge
- If you met **Keith** → you know the myth vs. reality gap
- If you met NONE of them → the Goddess's side is a black box

### Example: The Twin Converter's Identity
- If you met **Elena** → she theorizes about "paired anomalies"
- If you met **Wei** → he calculates the probability of dual summonings
- If you met **Lila** → she heard rumors from intelligence networks
- If you met NONE of them → the Twin Converter is a complete surprise

## Plot States

The game tracks which heroes you've encountered and adjusts dialogue accordingly:

| State | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Unknown** | Never encountered. No plot contribution. |
| **Met** | Encountered but neither corrupted nor killed. May appear again. |
| **Corrupted** | Joined your side. Delivers post-conversion dialogue. |
| **Dead** | Killed in combat. Other heroes may reference their death. |
| **Uncorrupted** | Was corrupted, then reverted by Twin Converter. Unique dialogue. |

## Key Plot Beats (Mandatory)

Certain plot points are **guaranteed** regardless of path:

1. **Chapter 1-2:** Isekai, Demon King summoning, Apostle's Touch revealed
2. **Chapter 3:** First corruption — the choice is real
3. **Chapter 10:** Twin Converter first mentioned (rumors)
4. **Chapter 15:** Twin Converter first direct encounter (Phase 2)
5. **Chapter 16:** Twin Converter enters Phase 3 (pursuit)
6. **Chapter 20:** Final main-game battle, path to capital opens
7. **Chapter 21-22:** Capital assault, all surviving heroes deployed
8. **Chapter 23:** Final confrontation with Twin Converter + Goddess/Demon King revelation
